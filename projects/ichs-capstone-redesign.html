Project

ICHS Capstone Redesign

University of Washington Informatics School

Software Engineer

Redesigned the International Community Health Services website end-to-end — from research and Figma prototypes to a live HTML/CSS implementation — to make care, job opportunities, and resources genuinely accessible to the communities ICHS serves.

Figma HTML CSS UX Research User Testing
GitHub Repo Live Website

The Problem

How might patients, job seekers, and donors easily understand and navigate ICHS services so they can access care, opportunities, and resources more effectively?

ICHS serves communities where many patients are not highly tech-savvy and prefer human-centered touchpoints — phone calls and in-person visits. But those channels don't scale: high call volumes, language barriers, and information overload mean critical details get lost. The existing website compounded the problem rather than solving it. Dense, cluttered layouts and poor information hierarchy created a stressful navigation experience that drove users away rather than guiding them toward care. The result was a growing gap between the services ICHS offered and the patients who needed them most.

The information solution was clear: restructure and redesign the website so that users across every level of digital fluency could find what they needed quickly, confidently, and without friction — turning the site into a genuine access point rather than a barrier.

What I Did

  • Conducted market and literature research on healthcare website best practices and design standards.
  • Collaborated with a cross-functional team and maintained ongoing communication with the project sponsor, International Community Health Services (ICHS), to gather design and engineering requirements.
  • Redesigned the website using Figma, translating sponsor needs and research insights into updated mockups.
  • Converted Figma designs into a functional website using HTML/CSS.
  • Conducted extensive user validation research, collecting feedback from sponsors and iterating on the design to incorporate findings into each successive version.

The Outcome

The project delivered a fully redesigned website grounded in real user needs. Through multiple rounds of sponsor feedback and user validation, the final product replaced a cluttered, hard-to-navigate interface with a clean, accessible experience tailored to the communities ICHS serves. Patients can now locate services and care information without friction, job seekers can find and apply to opportunities with clarity, and donors can understand the organization's mission at a glance. The redesign transformed the website from an afterthought into a genuine extension of ICHS's care — one that meets users where they are regardless of their technical background.
